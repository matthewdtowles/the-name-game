# Whose Name?

Play at [whosename.app](https://whosename.app).

A party-game companion for The Name Game. Everyone secretly writes down a famous
person or someone everyone in the room knows. Then players take turns guessing who
wrote which name. A correct guess pulls that player, along with their whole team, onto
your team. Play continues until one team holds everyone.

The app replaces the paper. Players join a room from their phones with a code or a QR
code and submit their names in secret. When the last name is in, the host's phone or a
TV shows the shuffled list. Nobody gets a head start from recognizing handwriting.

- No accounts. A player's identity lasts for one game.
- Guests can play in a mobile browser. The iOS and Android apps are optional.
- Any smart TV, Chromecast, or AirPlay screen can show the list.
